Basic Layout Guidelines

1-inch margins on all sides. Accessible font choices: Times New Roman 12pt, Arial 11pt, or Calibri 11pt. Double-spaced body text and references. Page number flush-right in the top header on every page starting with Page 1.

APA 7 Reference Rules & Formatting Examples

Book (Single Author)
In-Text Parenthetical: (Smith, 2020, p. 15)
In-Text Narrative: Smith (2020) highlighted that... (p. 15)
Reference List Entry: Smith, J. M. (2020). The psychology of learning: Critical perspectives. Academic Press. https://doi.org/10.1016/x1234
Note: Book titles are written in sentence case (capitalize only the first word, first word after a colon, and proper nouns). Omit the publisher city!
Journal Article (with DOI)
In-Text Parenthetical: (Jones & Taylor, 2019)
Reference List Entry: Jones, R. A., & Taylor, L. B. (2019). Cognitive behavioral therapy in practice. Journal of Clinical Psychology, 54(3), 112–125. https://doi.org/10.1037/0000123
Note: Article titles are in sentence case without quotation marks. The journal title and volume number are italicized.
Webpage or Online Article
In-Text Parenthetical: (World Health Organization, 2021)
Reference List Entry: World Health Organization. (2021, March 15). Mental health and wellness strategies. https://www.who.int/mental_health/strategies

Common APA 7 Mistakes to Avoid

Review this diagnostic checklist before submitting your final paper.

1. The "Running Head" Myth in Student Papers

In APA 7th Edition, student papers do NOT require a running head unless specifically requested by an instructor. Only a page number in the top right is needed.

Incorrect (APA 6th / Professional Only)

Header: RUNNING HEAD: PSYCHOLOGY OF LEARNING ........ 1

Correct APA 7 Student Form

Header: ............................................................................................ 1

2. Sentence Case for Article & Book Titles

In APA 7 reference entries, capitalize ONLY the first word of the title, the first word after a colon, and proper nouns. Do NOT use title case for article or book titles!

Incorrect Title Casing

Smith, J. (2020). The Psychology Of Learning: Critical Perspectives In Education.

Correct APA 7 Sentence Case

Smith, J. (2020). The psychology of learning: Critical perspectives in education.

3. Author In-Text Citations with "et al."

In APA 7, any source with 3 or more authors is abbreviated from the very first in-text citation using the first author's name and "et al."

Incorrect Form (3+ Authors)

First citation: (Miller, Davis, Wilson, & Taylor, 2018)

Correct APA 7 Form

First and subsequent citations: (Miller et al., 2018)

4. Formatting DOIs and URLs

DOIs and web URLs should be presented as full hyperlinks starting with https://. Do not place a period after the URL!

Incorrect Form

doi:10.1037/0000123. or http://dx.doi.org/10.1037/0000123.

Correct APA 7 Form

https://doi.org/10.1037/0000123

5. Eliminating Publisher Locations

APA 7 no longer requires the city and state/country of the publisher for book citations.

Incorrect Form

New York, NY: McGraw-Hill.

Correct APA 7 Form

McGraw-Hill.

6. Title Page Label & References Heading

The title on the References page and title page should be **Bolded and Centered**. Do not use "BIBLIOGRAPHY" or "WORKS CITED". Use **References** (or **Reference** if only citing one source).

Incorrect Form

BIBLIOGRAPHY (Unbolded, all caps)

Correct APA 7 Form

References (Centered, Bold, Title Case)
